# Kazakhstan Academy Pricing Hypothesis

This is a willingness-to-pay test, not a final price list. The goal is to learn
which buying unit clubs understand: academy license, per-session package, or
per-athlete report.

## Recommended Interview Offer

Lead with a paid pilot rather than free research:

- **Pilot package:** 4 assessment days over 6-8 weeks, up to 80 athletes, coach
  reports, aggregate summary, and one review meeting.
- **Pilot price to test:** 600,000-1,200,000 KZT total.
- **Success criterion:** the club agrees that repeated assessments and
  comparable reports are worth paying for before the 6-camera roadmap is ready.

## Pricing Models To Test

1. Academy license: 300,000-600,000 KZT/month per site for local-first software,
   standard reports, and support.
2. Assessment-day package: 200,000-400,000 KZT/day for operator-run screening.
3. Per-athlete report: 5,000-10,000 KZT/report, useful for private training
   centers but weaker for academy budgeting.

## Willingness-To-Pay Questions

- If this replaced manual screening notes for one age group, what budget owner
  would approve it: head coach, academy director, medical staff, or parents?
- Which price feels easier to approve: a monthly academy license, a paid
  assessment day, or per-athlete reports?
- What evidence would make this paid rather than a demo: repeatability, coach
  agreement, parent-facing report, physio export, or longitudinal tracking?

## Packaging Rules

- Sell the validated 4-camera assessment baseline first.
- Present 6-camera as the roadmap/moat, not the priced promise.
- Keep BLM/launcher out of the youth-academy SKU.
- Include C3D now where useful; add TRC/MOT once implemented for sports-science
  and clinic partnerships.
