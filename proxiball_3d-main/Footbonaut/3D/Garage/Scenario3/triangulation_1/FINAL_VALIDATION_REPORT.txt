ADDRESSING THE CAMERA POSITION DISCREPANCY
================================================================================

From discussion.txt analysis, the camera position mismatch is due to:

ROOT CAUSE: Coordinate frame mismatch
  - Extrinsics are correct in "TagFrame" (the coordinate system defined by
    your measured tag coordinates in Dimensions.txt)
  - Camera positions are calculated correctly in TagFrame
  - But TagFrame ≠ RoomFrame (your expected physical measurements)

This can happen due to:
  1. Axis convention differences (handedness, direction)
  2. Origin offset
  3. Rotation/reflection between frames
  4. Systematic measurement errors in tag coordinates

WHY TRIANGULATION STILL WORKS PERFECTLY:
  - All cameras share the same TagFrame
  - Ball 3D position will be in TagFrame
  - Triangulation only requires frame consistency, not physical correctness
