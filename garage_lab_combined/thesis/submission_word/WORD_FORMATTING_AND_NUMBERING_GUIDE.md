# Word Formatting and Numbering Guide (NU MSc ECE)

## 1) Global Document Setup
- Paper: A4
- Margins: 2.5 cm on all sides
- Font: Times New Roman, 12 pt, black
- Body line spacing: Double
- Paragraph alignment: Justified
- First-line indent: 1.25 cm
- Avoid decorative headers/borders

## 2) Pagination
- Title page: no displayed page number
- All other pages: page number centered top (or centered bottom if required by final department review)
- Follow handbook examples where Abstract starts on page 2

## 3) Required Element Order
1. Title page
2. Declaration form
3. Abstract (<=500 words)
4. Acknowledgements
5. Table of Contents
6. List of Abbreviations
7. List of Tables
8. List of Figures
9. Chapter 1 .. Chapter 9
10. Bibliography/References
11. Appendices

## 4) Heading Styles and Numbering
- Use Word `Heading 1` for chapter titles, with chapter-number prefix in title text:
  - `Chapter 1 - Introduction`
  - `Chapter 2 - Literature and Technology Review`
- Use Word `Heading 2` for sections:
  - `1.1 Background and Context`
  - `1.2 Problem Statement`
- Use Word `Heading 3` for subsections:
  - `1.4.1 ...`
- Numbering depth: up to subsection level (3 levels)

## 5) Figures, Tables, Equations
- Figure captions below figures: `Figure C.N: ...`
- Table captions above tables: `Table C.N: ...`
- Equation numbering by chapter: `(C.N)`
- Keep figure/table labels referenced in body text

## 6) Recommended Word Workflow
1. Open `THESIS_WORD_MASTER_75_85.md` in your editor and paste into Word.
2. Apply Heading styles and numbering.
3. Insert ToC / list of figures / list of tables from Word references tool.
4. Apply ASME reference formatting from `WORD_REFERENCE_STYLE_ASME.md`.
5. Run final grammar and consistency pass.
