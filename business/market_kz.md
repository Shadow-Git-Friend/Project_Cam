# Kazakhstan market map — football academies & facilities

Figures below marked *(verify)* are to be confirmed in discovery calls before
they appear in any external deck; do not quote them as facts.

## Segments (beachhead order)

1. **FIFA Talent Academy Kazakhstan** — one of ~39 FIFA Talent Academies
   worldwide; FIFA-driven talent-development standards create demand for
   objective measurement and give one reference customer national visibility.
2. **FC Kairat Academy (Almaty)** — the country's flagship club academy
   (UEFA-visible after Champions League qualification); an internal pilot
   pitch already exists in this repo (`kairat_pitch/`, RU speaker notes).
   Indoor facilities → fits the small-sided pilot scope exactly.
3. **Astana private academies** — FC Jenis and academy projects backed by
   local tech capital (Lomtadze, Turlov initiatives incl. Karaganda/Temirtau
   indoor arenas) *(verify current names/ownership)* — buyers who understand
   software and can move fast.
4. **KFF-affiliated regional academies & children's schools** — the volume
   tier; reachable only via KFF endorsement after validation phase.
5. **Adjacent:** futsal clubs (indoor by definition), regional olympic-reserve
   schools, and later Central Asia (UZ, KG) with the same climate/indoor logic.

## Sizing logic (bottom-up)

- Realistic serviceable market year 1–2: **10–30 academy sites** with budget
  authority ≥ $10k (club academies + private + FIFA/KFF flagships) *(verify
  count via KFF licensing list)*.
- Unit economics per site: $6–12k hardware+install (pass-through + margin) +
  $3–6k/yr software maintenance → $150–300k ARR at 30 sites on maintenance
  alone, before analytics packs.
- The scale tier (all KFF children's schools) is only addressable with a
  cheaper 2-camera "load-only" SKU — roadmap, not promise.

## Climate & facility reality (design center)

- Astana/Karaganda winters reach −30 °C: October–April training is indoor →
  fixed indoor installs have year-round utilization, and small-sided indoor
  pitches (~40×20 m) are exactly the scale our measured rig already handles.
- Indoor also removes the hardest CV conditions (rain, low sun, 60 m player
  distances) from the pilot — the honest engineering reason to start there.

## Localization requirements

- UI/reports: Kazakh + Russian mandatory, English for scouting export.
- Timestamps Asia/Almaty; units metric; ₸ pricing with USD hardware line
  items separated (FX pass-through).
- Data residency on academy premises satisfies both the KZ personal-data law
  (№ 94-V) and parent expectations for minors' footage.
